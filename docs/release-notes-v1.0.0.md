<h3 align="center">One mouse. Four computers.</h3>
<p align="center">Push your cursor off the edge of a screen and it hops to the next computer.<br>Keyboard, clipboard and files come along. All on your own network.</p>

## ✨ What you get

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sxhugo-cell/ScreenHopper/main/docs/media/features.jpg" alt="Edge hopping, keyboard, clipboard and files" width="100%">
</p>

- 🖱️ **Edge hopping:** arrange your screens to match your desk and push off any shared edge. It even hops between two other computers.
- ⌨️ **Keyboard follows:** type on whichever computer the cursor is on. Cmd and Ctrl can be swapped between Mac and Windows.
- 📋 **Shared clipboard:** text, rich text and images. Copied files travel too.
- 📁 **File transfer:** drop files or folders onto a computer. They land in a folder you choose.
- 🌗 **Dim and pause:** dim the screen that is not in control, or hit <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Esc</kbd> to take the cursor back.
- 🔒 **Private by design:** encrypted end to end (Noise, ChaCha20). No account, no cloud, no server.

## 🚀 Set up in four steps

1. Install Screen Hopper on each computer.
2. Enter the **same pairing key** on all of them. They find each other on your network.
3. Drag the screens in **Layout** to match your desk.
4. Push the cursor off the edge.
